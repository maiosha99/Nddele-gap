# Play Store Checklist

## Required Before Upload

- Build a signed Android App Bundle (`.aab`) from Android Studio.
- Replace the temporary launcher icon with final 512x512 store artwork.
- Create screenshots from a real device or emulator.
- Add a public privacy policy URL in Play Console.
- Complete the Data safety form.
- Complete the Content rating questionnaire.

## Suggested Listing

Full description:

```text
Needle Gap is a fast timing puzzle game built around precision, rhythm, and focus.

Watch the needle spin around the ring, tap when it lines up with an open gap, and close every gap to clear the level. Each phase introduces new movement patterns, changing speeds, drifting gaps, direction switches, and pulse timing.

Features:
- 150 handcrafted progression levels
- Clean neon arcade style
- Simple one-tap controls
- Lives and retry system
- Best score saved on device
```
