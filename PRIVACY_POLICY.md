# Privacy Policy for Needle Gap

Effective date: July 30, 2026

Needle Gap does not directly collect, store, sell, or share personal information.

The game stores only local gameplay progress, such as best score, on your device using local browser storage inside the app. This data is not sent to the developer or to any server.

The app uses Google AdMob to show rewarded advertisements. Google may collect data such as device identifiers, ad interactions, diagnostics, and approximate information needed to serve and measure ads. Please review Google's advertising privacy information before publishing.

The app may access the internet to load ads and web fonts used by the game interface. No account, contacts, photos, microphone, or camera access is requested by the app.

If analytics, leaderboards, cloud saves, or other online services are added in a future version, this policy should be updated before that version is published.
